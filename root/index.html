About Me

Some of the work I do is 2D and 3D art which involves modeling and texturing, graphic design, voice acting, game development in Unity, animation, and I am currently learning how to program in Java and HTML.


I love video games and am a huge fan of Sonic the Hedgehog, who is featured in the model presented above.

Q: What's your ambition?

I want to be a game developer working on one of the franchises I love officially.

Q: What do you like to do in your free time? Specific hobbies?

I'm a huge gamer, obviously. I also love to draw, create models, and listen to music!

Q: Where do you see yourself in 10 years?

Hoping to see myself working for SEGA or Nintendo.
